13. Business Recommendations

  1. Review the operating practices of Outlet 1, which recorded the highest net revenue in the retained dataset. Compare staffing, product mix and operating processes with the other outlets.
  2. Prioritise a margin and cost review for Product 2, the highest gross-profit product. Check whether its pricing, availability and promotion strategy can be maintained.

14. Conclusion

The analysis provides a structured view of FreshFlow's recorded sales performance and the quality of the available transaction data. The financial, outlet, product and daily summaries can support further business review. Decisions should be based on both revenue and gross profit rather than sales volume alone.

15. Limitations

  • The analysis covers only the supplied 30-day transaction window and cannot establish annual seasonality.
  • Gross profit is not net profit because rent, payroll and other operating expenses are not included.
  • Discount scenarios hold quantity and unit cost constant and do not estimate customer demand response.
  • Correlation does not prove a causal relationship.
  • Transaction data alone cannot establish inventory waste or stock-flow reconciliation. A daily inventory companion file is required.
  • Flagged outliers require human investigation and should not be treated as confirmed errors.
